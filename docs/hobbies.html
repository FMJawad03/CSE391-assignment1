BEYOND THE CLASSROOM

Things That Fascinate Me

From challenging game worlds to the mysteries of space and the history of human innovation.

01

Souls & Souls-like Games

I enjoy Souls and Souls-like games for their challenging combat, intricate world design and environmental storytelling. These games teach patience, perseverance, observation and adaptability, encouraging players to learn from mistakes rather than give up. Studying enemy attack patterns, mastering difficult boss fights and gradually improving through repeated attempts make every victory feel rewarding. I also appreciate exploring interconnected worlds, uncovering hidden areas, and piecing together the stories behind their mysterious settings.

02

Music

Music is an important part of how I relax, focus and enjoy my free time. I particularly enjoy video game soundtracks and anime soundtracks, especially pieces that create a memorable atmosphere or convey emotions without words. I appreciate how music can make a scene more powerful, bring back memories and give fictional worlds their own distinct identity. Some soundtracks remain memorable long after the games or anime themselves are finished.

03

Astronomy & The Cosmos

The universe is filled with mysteries that continue to fascinate humanity, from distant galaxies and black holes to the origins of space itself. I really enjoy learning about celestial objects, the vastness of the cosmos and the discoveries that have expanded our understanding of the universe. There is something captivating about how much we have uncovered and how many questions remain unanswered.

04

The Evolution of Inventions

Human innovation has transformed the world, turning simple ideas into inventions that have changed how we live. I like learning how inventions have evolved over time, from early tools and machines to aircraft and modern technology. Understanding the ideas behind these developments and how they influenced society gives me a deeper appreciation for human creativity and progress.

05

Ancient Civilizations & Their Stories

The ancient world holds countless stories of remarkable civilizations, their cultures, achievements and mysteries. I am interested in discovering how people lived, built great cities, developed their traditions and overcame the challenges of their time. Exploring their history offers a glimpse into the past and helps me understand how ancient societies shaped the modern world.

06

Technology & How Things Work

Behind the devices and systems we use every day are fascinating ideas and mechanisms waiting to be understood. I like exploring different areas of technology, from computers and software to electronics and engineering, to discover how things work. Learning how individual components come together to create practical solutions keeps my curiosity alive and encourages me to explore unfamiliar topics.
